Phantom Failure Detector

When a payment shows as "failed" at checkout but actually clears the bank later, everyone loses. The customer thinks their order failed, the merchant misses out on a sale, and the money has already left the account. This model spots these phantom failures instantly, identifying which "failed" transactions actually went through long before the bank ledger updates.
